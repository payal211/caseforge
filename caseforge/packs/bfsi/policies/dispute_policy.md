# Card dispute and fraud investigation policy

## Unauthorized transaction criteria
A disputed transaction is treated as unauthorized when the customer denies initiating it and at least two independent risk signals are present, such as a new device, an unusual country, abnormal amount or rapid velocity. Fraud confirmation requires analyst approval.

## Card testing and velocity
Several small transactions within one hour, especially micro-amounts below 10 followed by a large purchase, indicate card testing. Treat card testing followed by a high-value purchase as probable fraud and block the card immediately.

## Device and identity signals
A device never previously seen on the account is a strong risk signal. A device identifier linked to three or more accounts indicates a compromised or fraudulent device farm and must be escalated to the fraud intelligence team.

## Cross-border transactions
A transaction in a country that differs from the customer's home country and has no prior history is high risk. If the customer has previous activity in that country on a known device, treat the transaction as lower risk and verify with the customer.

## Abnormal amount
An amount more than four standard deviations above the customer's historical average requires step-up verification. Amounts between two and four deviations warrant analyst review.

## Customer liability and refund
When fraud is approved, the customer is refunded within 10 business days and the card is blocked and reissued. Customers carry zero liability for confirmed unauthorized transactions reported within 3 days.

## Escalation thresholds
Risk score of 70 or above: recommend block and refund. Score between 40 and 69: escalate to a senior analyst and request customer verification. Score below 40: decline the dispute and refer the customer to the merchant dispute process. Every recommendation requires human approval.
