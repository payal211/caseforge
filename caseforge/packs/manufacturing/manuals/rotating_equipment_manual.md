# Rotating equipment maintenance manual (pumps and compressors)

## Bearing wear
Symptoms: vibration and bearing temperature drift upward together. Vibration above 4.5 mm/s indicates advanced wear. Typical root causes are lubrication breakdown, misalignment or contamination. Plan corrective maintenance within 48 hours.
Step: Isolate and lock out the machine
Step: Inspect the bearing housing and take a grease sample
Step: Replace drive-end and non-drive-end bearings
Step: Realign the coupling and re-lubricate
Part: BRG-6205-2RS | Deep groove ball bearing | qty 2
Part: GRS-EP2-400 | EP2 bearing grease cartridge | qty 1
Estimated downtime: 6 hours

## Seal leak and pressure loss
Symptoms: discharge pressure falls while motor current rises. Typical root causes are mechanical seal leakage or impeller wear. Inspect at the next shift and plan seal replacement.
Step: Isolate and depressurise the line
Step: Inspect the mechanical seal and impeller for wear
Step: Replace the mechanical seal and gaskets
Part: SEAL-MS-35 | Mechanical seal 35 mm | qty 1
Part: GSK-KIT-12 | Gasket kit | qty 1
Estimated downtime: 4 hours

## Cavitation
Symptoms: vibration rises while discharge pressure drops and fluctuates. Check suction conditions and inlet strainer.
Step: Inspect suction line and strainer
Step: Verify NPSH margin
Part: STR-80 | Inlet strainer element | qty 1
Estimated downtime: 2 hours

## General inspection
If drift cannot be classified, schedule a visual inspection and vibration spectrum analysis at the next planned stop.
