# Claims handling policy

## Abnormal claim amount
A claim far above the policy's historical average requires documentation review. Amounts more than four standard deviations above average need an adjuster to verify invoices before payout.

## High value claim senior review limit
Any claim of 25,000 or more must be reviewed by a senior adjuster regardless of other signals.

## New provider
A provider that has never billed the policy is a risk signal. Verify licence and address, and compare invoices with provider history.

## Burst of claims
Several claims on one policy within a few hours indicate possible claim splitting or staged losses. Treat the burst as a strong signal when combined with a new provider.

## Shared payout bank account
A payout account linked to three or more unrelated policies indicates a possible fraud ring. Hold payment and refer to the special investigations unit.

## Out of state claim
A claim filed from a state different from the policy home state needs location verification, unless the policy has travel cover.

## Escalation thresholds
Risk score 70 or above: refer to special investigations and hold payout. Score 40 to 69: escalate to a senior adjuster. Score below 40: fast-track payout. Every recommendation requires human approval.
